Frequently asked questions

Is STEMBridge Speaks really free? +

Yes. Conversation practice, the grammar course, and the dictionary are all free, with no subscription or hidden paywall.

Which languages can I practice? +

German, English, Spanish, Mandarin, and Russian for conversation practice and the structured grammar course. Arabic is also available for conversation practice.

Can I practice speaking out loud, not just typing? +

Yes — speak into your microphone and the AI transcribes, responds, and speaks back to you in the target language, adjusted to your proficiency level from A1 to C2.
