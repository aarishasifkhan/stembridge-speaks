Free · No subscription · 5 languages, real conversation

Learn to actually speak the language, not just pass the quiz.

Grammar apps teach you the rules. STEMBridge Speaks makes you say the sentence out loud, in a real scenario — a restaurant, a hotel, a shop — and corrects you mid-conversation, the way a patient native speaker would.

Three steps, every time

No lesson plans to schedule, no levels to unlock. Just open a scenario and start talking, in whichever of the five languages you're learning.

01

Pick a language and a scene

German, English, Spanish, Mandarin, or Russian — in a restaurant, hotel, shop, or on the street.

02

Say it out loud

Speak or type, mistakes included — that's the point.

03

Get corrected, mid-conversation

A plain-language fix, then the conversation just keeps going.

Five languages to start

We picked languages that are both widely spoken and genuinely difficult to get conversationally comfortable in — more are coming as the project grows.

🇩🇪

German

Cases, gender, and word order that trip up even confident learners.

🇬🇧

English

Irregular verbs and prepositions that don't follow the rules.

🇪🇸

Spanish

Ser vs. estar, and conjugations that change with every subject.

🇨🇳

Mandarin

Tones, measure words, and a completely different sentence logic.

🇷🇺

Russian

Six cases and verb aspect that don't exist in English at all.

Where you'll practice

Real situations you'd actually run into, not vocabulary lists.

🍽️

Restaurant

🗺️

Directions

🛍️

Shopping

🏨

Hotel

Check in, ask about your room, sort out a reservation mix-up.

Built by a student, kept free on purpose.

I'm Aarish, and I started STEMBridge Speaks because I was learning German myself and got tired of language apps that either taught grammar in isolation or charged a monthly fee for basic conversation practice. This is part of STEMBridge, a free STEM and language education initiative.
