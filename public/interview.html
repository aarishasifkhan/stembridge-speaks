Interview Rehearsal

Practice a real interview before the real thing — in a foreign language, or as English behavioral coaching with STAR-method feedback.

The STAR Method

  • Situation — set the context: what was going on?
  • Task — what were you responsible for?
  • Action — what did you specifically do?
  • Result — what happened, ideally with a measurable outcome?

Common behavioral questions

  • "Tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult coworker."
  • "Describe a situation where you had to meet a tight deadline."
  • "Give an example of a mistake you made and how you handled it."
