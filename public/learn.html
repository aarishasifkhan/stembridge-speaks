How the course is structured

Each language's course is split into two tracks: five foundational lessons covering the basics you'd need for real conversation, followed by eleven advanced lessons — grammar, sentence structure, and usage topics matched to what a Grade 9-12 curriculum would cover. Every lesson ends in a five-question quiz, and every example sentence has its own pronunciation audio, generated specifically for that lesson.
