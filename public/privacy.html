Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you use the Conversational Practice tool, your typed or spoken messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. If you submit feedback (👍/👎, with an optional comment), we store the exchange (your message, the tutor's reply, the scenario, and language) in a database to help us improve the product.

What we don't collect

We do not require an account to use the practice tool, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information through the practice interface itself.

Third-party services

  • Google Gemini API — processes your messages to generate tutor responses and audio.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores feedback submissions.
  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces used on this site.

If this site displays advertisements (via Google AdSense), Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for basic functionality and, if advertising is enabled, for ad personalization by Google and its partners.

Questions about this policy can be sent to thestembridgechannel@gmail.com.
